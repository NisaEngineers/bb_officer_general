On negative marking strategy

Each wrong answer costs −0.25. You need to be at least 80% confident before answering. If you're between 2 options, guess. If you have no idea, skip. Attempting all 100 questions blindly costs you more than skipping 10–15 uncertain ones.

What to bring on exam day
  • Admit card (printed, 2 copies)
  • National ID or Birth Certificate
  • Black ink ball-point pen
  • Pencil (for rough work)

No mobile phones, calculators, smartwatches, or electronic devices allowed.

Time management during the exam

You have 60 minutes for 100 questions — 36 seconds per question. Move fast on Bangla and GK (factual). Spend a bit more time on Math. If stuck, mark and move on. Return to skipped questions in the last 10 minutes.

High-yield banking terms to memorize
  • CRR = 4%
  • SLR = 13% (conventional)
  • Repo rate (check latest)
  • BB established 1971
  • BB Order = Presidential Order 127, 1972
  • 1st private bank = AB Bank (1982)
  • SWIFT, CIB, BACH, BEFTN
  • CAMELS rating
  • Lender of last resort
